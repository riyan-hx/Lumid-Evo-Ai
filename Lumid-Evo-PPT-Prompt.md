# Prompt: generate the Lumid Evo pitch deck (for any AI slide tool)

How to use: copy everything below the line into the AI tool (Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Canva, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.). Attach the images from the `pitch-assets/` folder where the **IMAGE SLOTS** say so. Ask for a downloadable .pptx.

---

## ROLE
You are a senior presentation designer and pitch writer. Build an **8-slide, 16:9 pitch deck** (a 6-minute talk) for **Lumid Evo**, an everyday problem-solving companion for students, exam aspirants and young professionals in Kerala, India.

## THE ONE IDEA (this drives every slide)
The deck is **solution-first**. The problem gets **one slide**. Everything else shows **how Evo solves real problems, in plain words, for real people**. A viewer should understand in five seconds: *"Someone is stuck → Evo finds the cause → gives one small step → checks it helped."* Prefer a concrete example over an explanation. If a sentence is not needed, delete it.

## RULES FOR THE COPY (strict)
- **Short.** Titles: one line if possible (max two). Body lines: max 15 words. No paragraphs, no bullet lists longer than four items.
- **Plain language.** Write for a college student and a busy panel. No jargon: say "silly mistakes", not "attentional errors"; "one small step", not "behavioural activation".
- **Use only the facts and numbers written below.** Do not add, round, change or invent any statistic, quote, name or source. If you want more evidence, leave it out.
- **Every example person is fictional.** Label them "fictional demo people" where shown.
- **English only.** No Malayalam text.
- **Never claim Evo diagnoses, treats or has proven outcomes.** Evo has **no outcome data yet**; say so where shown.
- Do not imply the psychologist console or institution dashboard exists. They are **Next** (pilot), not built.

## DESIGN SYSTEM (strict)
- **Style:** minimal, professional, calm. Swiss/editorial. Generous white space, thin hairline rules, no shadows, no gradients (except the cover orb), no clip-art, no emojis, no stock photos of people.
- **Font:** **Helvetica** everywhere (fallback Arial). Titles Bold 28 pt. Big numbers Bold 54–58 pt. Body 12.5–15 pt. Captions and sources 9–10 pt. Section kickers 10 pt Bold, UPPERCASE, letter-spaced, Deep green.
- **Colours (Lumid Dashboard):** Forest `#242E21` (text on white, dark slides), **Lime `#88D95F` (the only accent, use sparingly)**, Deep green `#007950` (kickers, names, links, "helped" ticks), Soft lime `#E4F7D6` (highlight bands), Wash `#F6F8F5` (neutral bands), Hairline `#DDE3DD`, Muted `#6B7571` (captions), Body `#3A4440`. White background on content slides; Forest background on the cover only.
- **Layout grid:** 13.333 × 7.5 in. Left margin 0.7 in. Kicker top-left (UPPERCASE), title below, content from y ≈ 1.95 in. Footer: "Lumid Evo" left, page number right, 9 pt Muted.
- **Columns:** a top hairline (Forest) above each column, then heading, then text. Highlight bands are a Soft lime or Wash rectangle with one line of text.
- **Icons:** thin line icons only (Feather stroke style), Forest or Deep green, 0.4–0.55 in.
- **No charts.** Use big numbers instead.
- **Speaker notes:** add the notes given per slide. They are the spoken script.
