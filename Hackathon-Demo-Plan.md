## Rules that win judges
- **Say built vs next every time.** Never click through a psychologist console or institution dashboard; they do not exist yet.
- **Say "fictional demo people" once** at the first sign-in, and "scripted coach so it works the same every time; a live AI model is next" once at Anu's chat.
- **Show the result, not the feature:** the 85% → 35% bar, the silly-mistakes bar, "It helped".
- Don't type live. Tap the chips; they are the whole conversation.

## If asked
- *Is this therapy?* No. A first step and a bridge; it hands over to Tele-MANAS or a psychologist and never diagnoses.
- *Does it work?* Methods have trial evidence (Friendship Bench 14% vs 50%). Evo has no outcome data yet; weeks 3–4 of the pilot measure it.
- *Is the AI real?* Demo replies are scripted; the safety checks are the same ones a live model runs under.
- *Privacy?* Private by default; nothing shared without consent; chat content never goes to a contact or institution.
